Governance

Usage principles

  • Formal contexts Use the full horizontal lockup on reports, decks, letterhead, and website header.
  • Compact spaces Use the BFI monogram for favicon, app icon, nav collapse, and document stamps.
  • Subtle moments Icon-only mark for watermarks, loading states, and diagram corners.
  • Clear space Minimum padding = height of the symbol on all sides. Never crowd the mark.
  • Color Default to black or midnight on warm white. Reverse to warm white on midnight or charcoal only.
  • Integrity Never stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, glow, gradient-fill, or place on busy photography.

Restrictions

Do not use

  • Neon, gradient rainbow, or tech-glow treatments on the symbol
  • Replacing the wordmark with rounded startup sans typefaces
  • Literal mountains, rockets, brains, globes, or university seals
  • Gold overload or crypto-style metallic effects
  • Distorted aspect ratios or off-brand colors
  • Any third-party logo, crest, or copied mark — all geometry here is original BFI work
