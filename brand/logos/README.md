# BFI Logo System

## BFI

The master institution mark should feel like discovery, truth, and academic
timelessness. Preferred directions:

1. Minimal circle with a B or aperture cut by negative space
2. Monolith rectangle with a central cut
3. Compass or north-star knowledge symbol
4. Fractal expansion mark for research discovery

## BR3N

AI product identity: black cube, geometry, network, chrome, and glass.

## SOLGLIA

Business intelligence identity: hexagon, data nodes, precision, and executive
dashboard energy.

## OLTRE

Luxury design identity: simple O, marble, stone, thin line, and quiet confidence.

## Rules

- Do not copy Gucci, Ferrari, Apple, OpenAI, Anthropic, Stripe, or any other
  proprietary logo.
- Use their design principles only: restraint, spacing, craft, and clarity.
- Every mark must work at favicon size.
- Every mark must have a one-color version.
