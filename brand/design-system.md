# BFI Design System Master

## Brand position

**BFI - Bowers Frontier Institute**

Discovering what humanity does not yet know.

BFI is luxury academic research, not corporate software. The brand should feel
timeless, sparse, intelligent, and deeply crafted: Apple-level interface
discipline, Anthropic/OpenAI clarity, Stripe precision, BlackRock
professionalism, and the restraint of high luxury houses without copying their
proprietary marks.

## Commercial ecosystem

BFI research creates the source material for commercialization:

1. BFI - research institution
2. BR3N - AI products and autonomous agent systems
3. SOLGLIA - business intelligence and executive dashboards
4. OLTRE - luxury design, architecture, and fashion-adjacent systems
5. Future companies - additional commercialization vehicles

The ecosystem should share a common layout language, typography discipline, and
component model. Differences come from iconography, imagery, accent materials,
and product tone rather than unrelated visual systems.

## Typography

Use an elegant old-style serif for wordmarks/headlines and a clean sans-serif for
interface text.

- Headline: Cormorant Garamond or EB Garamond
- Body: Inter, Neue Haas Grotesk, SF Pro, or IBM Plex Sans fallback
- Mono: IBM Plex Mono or a restrained technical mono

Rules:

- Use a maximum of two font families in any single product surface.
- Use wide spacing in wordmarks and labels.
- Let hierarchy come from scale, spacing, and weight instead of color.

## Color palette

- Black: `#050505`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Warm white: `#F7F6F2`
- Stone: `#CFCFC8`
- Graphite: `#1B1B1B`
- Gold accent: `#B89A5D`

Use almost no color. Accent should feel like a material detail, not decoration.

## Cursor design principles

- Build mobile-first, then desktop.
- Favor whitespace over decoration.
- Limit each screen to one primary action.
- Use subtle animations in the 150-250ms range.
- Design every component as reusable.
- Keep a cohesive ecosystem across BFI, BR3N, OLTRE, and SOLGLIA.
- Do not copy proprietary luxury logos or type treatments; use principles only.
