# ECB FX history attribution

Source: ECB statistics.

This directory stores a versioned research extract of the European Central Bank
euro foreign exchange reference rates, plus derived USD-major crosses and the
paper-only walk-forward evaluation artifacts.

The ECB allows reproduction provided the source is acknowledged. EURUSD in the
CSV is the ECB USD reference rate. Other pairs are derived crosses and are not
official ECB USD quotes. Missing TARGET business days are recorded, not filled.

Paper/research use only. These files are not a trading feed, live market data
product, or execution record.
