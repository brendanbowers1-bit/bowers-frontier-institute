# Branding Rules

BFI is the parent research institution. BR3N, SOLGLIA, and OLTRE are commercial
or product expressions beneath the BFI system.

## BFI

- Luxury academic research, not corporate SaaS.
- Huge whitespace, cinematic imagery, restrained copy.
- Almost no color beyond black, white, warm white, stone, graphite, and subtle
  gold.
- Voice: precise, minimal, and civilizational.

## BR3N

- AI products and autonomous agents.
- Black, chrome, glass, geometry.
- Voice: technical, calm, research-governed.

## SOLGLIA

- Enterprise intelligence and executive dashboards.
- Dark mode, precision, structured data, terminal-grade clarity.
- Voice: decisive, analytical, executive.

## OLTRE

- Luxury design, architecture, and fashion-adjacent work.
- Black, marble, stone, thin lines.
- Voice: quiet confidence.
